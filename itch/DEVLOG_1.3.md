# Devlog draft: After Class 1.3 ♡

**Title:** After Class 1.3 — a longer week, a summer scrapbook, and new friends ♡
**Type:** Update

---

Hi everyone! ♡

After Class just got its biggest update yet. If you already own it, the itch app updates it for you, or you can download it again from the game page.

**A longer last week of term**
- Every day now has homeroom, a group lunch on the roof (technically illegal, spiritually required), and a group chat that buzzes every night.
- Each girl's afternoons are longer. Hina's family bakery, Rei's grandpa's camera, Sayo's sketchbook. And you can finally take Rei's picture, for once.

**New classmates**
- **Daichi**, Hina's neighbor, who has a napkin map of the beach.
- **Kanda-sensei**, our very tired homeroom teacher.
- **Nurse Mori**, who always makes two cups of tea.
- **Mrs. Okada** at the konbini by the bridge, who always asks "same as yesterday?"

**The Summer Scrapbook**
There are 38 memories to collect: Hina's summer list, Rei's photos, Sayo's sketches and little keepsakes. They come from the choices you make and the afternoons you spend. One week can't hold all of them, so you'll have to come back. ♡ Fill enough of it and there's a special photo waiting for you at the end.

**A fresh coat of paint**
- A brand new pastel look: notebook-paper menus, hearts everywhere, and a little heart cursor.
- A new icon, and the Office has a lovely new voice for the school announcements.

**Free demo**
The first week is still free to play in your browser.

Thank you so much for playing. Summer break is only five days away!

See you after class ♡
— Stagworks
