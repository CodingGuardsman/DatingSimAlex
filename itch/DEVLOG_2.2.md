# Devlog: After Class 2.2 ♡ (draft, not posted yet)

**Title:** After Class 2.2 — The Mirror Update ♡
**Type:** Major Update

---

Hi everyone! ♡

After Class 2.2 is out! If you already own it, the itch app updates it for you, or you can download it again from the game page.

**The girls pay more attention to you now**
We've been reading your messages, and a lot of you told us the girls felt like they *really* knew you. So we taught them to notice even more: how you play, what you pick, and how long you take to pick it. They remember. That's what friends do. ♡

**A livelier school**
- The girls react more when they're happy, flustered or surprised. Keep an eye out for little hearts!
- The first days of the week are a little sweeter and a little easier to follow.

**The second week has new places to go**
There are new rooms, new scenes and a few new surprises in the rerun. We won't spoil any of them. Some of them work best in the desktop version.

**The Office has a few updates of its own**
- Updated Terms of Attendance. Please read them carefully. (Nobody does.)
- Basic gamepad support, for those of you who like to play from the couch.
- A few scenes ask to use your microphone. They always ask first, and saying no is always fine. Nothing ever leaves your computer.

**Free demo**
The first week is still free to play in your browser, with the sweeter, smoother Monday and Tuesday.

Thank you so much for playing, and for coming back. We see you. ♡

See you after class ♡
— Stagworks
