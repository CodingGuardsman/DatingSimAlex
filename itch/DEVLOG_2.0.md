# Devlog: After Class 2.0 ♡ (posted 2026-11-01: https://stagworks.itch.io/after-class/devlog/1744981)

**Title:** After Class 2.0 — mornings, a second term, and so much more of everything ♡
**Type:** Major Update

---

Hi everyone! ♡

After Class 2.0 is out, and it's the biggest update yet: the game is now about **twice as long**, with a first playthrough running around **3–4 hours**. If you already own it, the itch app updates it for you, or you can download it again from the game page.

**Mornings!**
Every day now starts with twenty minutes before homeroom. Stop by Amamiya Bakery and meet Hina's little brother Sora (he will test whether you're a ninja). Watch the 7:42 train cross the river with Rei. Or cut through the courtyard under the infirmary window, where Sayo sometimes drops paper airplanes.

**Make the week yours**
- Write your own line on Hina's summer list. She's going to hold you to it.
- Borrow Rei's camera and take a photo of your own.
- Sayo will ask you to draw something for her. Anything at all. She keeps everything she's given.

**A second term**
Some of you reached summer and asked what happens next. Hoshimi Academy is opening its doors for a second term! There's a lot to discover, and we won't spoil any of it here. ♡

**More endings, more memories**
- There are now **seven endings**, and a new Endings page in your Summer Scrapbook so you can keep track.
- The Scrapbook has grown from 38 to **69 memories**.

**The Office has been busy too**
- New school announcements, in the same lovely voice.
- Achievements! Because you deserve to be rewarded for spending time with us.
- A little survey about your week. It only takes a minute. ★★★★★

**Free demo**
The first week is still free to play in your browser, and it now has the new mornings too.

Thank you so much for playing, and for coming back. It means more than you know.

See you after class ♡
— Stagworks
